Run `compliance`, `attention`, `overdue`, `cert-renewals` and `valves-due` with `--json`. For each finding say the fix (book the inspection, test the valve, close the anomaly, record the certificate). List what to book with the inspection body this week and offer `/draft-booking` for each. Write it to `drafts/monday-integrity-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Change nothing without a yes.

Ambiguous names list the candidates and stop: ask which one. Never send anything; drafts stay in `drafts/`.
