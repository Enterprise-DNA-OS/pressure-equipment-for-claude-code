---
description: Record a new certificate of inspection from the inspection body
---

Run `npm run integrity -- certify <equipment> <certificate no> --expires=YYYY-MM-DD [--issued=YYYY-MM-DD] [--by=..] [--conditions=".."]`. Copy the number, dates and any conditions exactly as printed on the certificate. Certificates cannot be deleted.

Ambiguous names list the candidates and stop: ask which one. Never send anything; drafts stay in `drafts/`.
