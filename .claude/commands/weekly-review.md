description: The weekly review written from the findings, the due list, corrosion and the anomalies
---

Run `site-summary`, `compliance`, `attention`, `remaining-life`, `open-anomalies` and `bad-actors` with `--json`. Write the review to `drafts/weekly-review-YYYY-MM-DD.md`: one line per site, compliance findings with the rule, what falls due in the next month, items with short remaining life, open anomalies by severity, and the worst three items. Nothing sends.

Ambiguous names list the candidates and stop: ask which one. Never send anything; drafts stay in `drafts/`.
